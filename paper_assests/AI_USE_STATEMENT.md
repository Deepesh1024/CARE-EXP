# AI Use Statement

In accordance with ICLR 2027 guidelines, we disclose the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) during the preparation of this manuscript. LLMs were utilized for:
- Research ideation and hypothesis refinement (e.g., formalizing the sequential intervention framework).
- Experimental methodology discussion and interpretation of empirical audits.
- Code generation, debugging, and visualization script creation (matplotlib/pandas).
- Scientific writing assistance, manuscript structuring, and drafting of figure captions.

All conceptual designs, final scientific claims, and interpretations were rigorously audited and verified by the authors. No experimental results, numbers, or citations were generated or hallucinated by AI; all numerical assets are mathematically derived from execution of the benchmark codebase.
