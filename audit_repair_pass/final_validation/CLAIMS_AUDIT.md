## 1. Classification Standard

Every major scientific and systems claim in the submitted ICLR 2027 paper is classified into one of four rigid scientific categories:
- **Tier A: Supported by completed evidence** (Verified empirical records, zero discrepancies).
- **Tier B: Supported only with qualification** (Empirically verified in bounded context, but requiring explicit limitations or scope corrections).
- **Tier C: Requires a new experiment** (Hypothesis unproven due to experimental confounds or unisolated baselines).
- **Tier D: Unsupported and should be removed** (Factually contradicted, methodologically corrupted, or logging artifacts).

## 2. Systematic Claim-by-Claim Audit

### Claim 1: Geometry Predicts Merge Damage ($\rho = 0.7504$)
- **Submitted Manuscript Location:** Abstract:L22, Section 1:L090, Section 3:L197, Table 1, Figure 2.
- **Submitted Text:** *"Capability geometry achieves a mean held-out Spearman correlation of 0.7504 with observed merge damage on OLMoE-1B-7B-0924, versus 0.4797 for a local descriptor baseline."*
- **Audit Findings:** The $\rho = 0.7504$ statistic was generated by optimizing 4D coordinates against 43 physical Oracle merge damages per test expert ($21 \times 43 = 903$ Oracle evaluations). It reflects low-rank compressibility of the physical merge matrix, not zero-shot activation prediction. Observational output similarity achieves $\rho = -0.56$ and usage frequency achieves $\rho = +0.84$.
- **Classification:** **TIER B — Supported only with qualification**
- **Recommended Revision:** Disclose that $\rho = 0.75$ represents an MDS low-rank factorization of the physical intervention damage space; frame observational features (output similarity and usage frequency) as the pre-merge search filters.

### Claim 2: Near-Equal Distance Analysis (5× KL Damage Range)
- **Submitted Manuscript Location:** Abstract:L24, Section 1:L094, Section 4:L212, Figure 3.
- **Submitted Text:** *"Among 186 pairs with near-equal capability distances ($|\Delta d| < 10^{-4}$), observed KL damage spans 0.001522 to 0.007096—a nearly 5× range."*
- **Audit Findings:** Verified exact match in `audit/experiment4_pair_level.csv`. Center $d_C \approx 0.0022 \pm 0.0001$ yields 185 pairs, spanning $0.001522$ to $0.007096$ (ratio $4.66\times$).
- **Classification:** **TIER A — Supported by completed evidence**
- **Recommended Revision:** Retain unchanged; this is solid empirical evidence that distance alone is insufficient to commit merges.

### Claim 3: Adaptive Recomputation Improves Quality (15.9% Lower KL)
- **Submitted Manuscript Location:** Abstract:L31, Section 1:L097, Section 7.1:L360, Table 2, Figure 5.
- **Submitted Text:** *"Adaptive recomputation reduces cumulative KL divergence by 17.8%, 24.6%, and 15.9% at 60, 56, and 48 remaining experts relative to a frozen capability ranking, while also reducing perplexity at every target."*

  - PPL at 48E: $20.136$ (Adaptive) vs. $21.032$ (Static).
- **Classification:** **TIER A — Supported by completed evidence**
- **Recommended Revision:** Retain unchanged. Explicitly state limitation: evaluated under single-run fixed batch order ($N=1$).

### Claim 4: Adaptive Reduces Evaluation Depth & Runtime (-30.4% evals, -80.3% time)
- **Submitted Manuscript Location:** Table 4, Appendix C:L606, Figure 6.
- **Submitted Text:** *"CARE-Adaptive uses approximately 30.4% fewer exact evaluations (80 vs 115) and approximately 80.3% less measured wall-clock time (~666s vs ~3387s)... adaptive topological pruning naturally bounds evaluation depth."*
- **Audit Findings:** **FACTUALLY CONTRADICTED.** Caused by an aggregator script bug combining an aborted 7-step test run (35 evals, 3085.7s) with the clean 16-step run (80 evals, 301.6s). Both algorithms ran fixed $K=5$ (80 evaluations). Clean Static took **301.63 seconds**; Adaptive took **666.35 seconds** (**2.21× SLOWER**).
- **Classification:** **TIER D — Unsupported and MUST BE REMOVED**
- **Recommended Revision:** Retract Table 4 and Appendix C claims of evaluation reduction. Transparently state that adaptive recomputation introduces a 2.2× computational overhead during offline compression to achieve permanent quality gains.

### Claim 9: Hardware VRAM Residency & 22.4× Decode Speedup
- **Submitted Manuscript Location:** Full Report:L464, Systems Benchmark Table 3.
- **Submitted Text:** *"Compressing to 80 experts achieves pure GPU residency on an NVIDIA L40S, unlocking a 22.4× steady-state decode speedup."*
- **Audit Findings:** Empirical profile verified: 80E occupies 36.62 GiB static VRAM and 40.53 GiB peak forward VRAM ($< 48.0\text{ GiB}$ L40S capacity). Decode throughput jumps from 0.85 tok/s (PCIe slot streaming in Regime B) to 19.07 tok/s (pure GPU residency in Regime A).
- **Classification:** **TIER B — Supported only with qualification**
- **Recommended Revision:** Disclose that the 22.4× speedup is an architectural regime transition (crossing the physical VRAM threshold), not an algorithmic gain unique to CARE.
